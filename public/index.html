    What is a stamp?

    After a check you can save the verdict on Arc with one wallet approval, about 0.001 USDC. It is a permanent public note that someone marked this payment. Anyone can stamp, so a stamp shows who claimed it, not what is true. The check is what proves it.

    What Assay cannot do

    It cannot tell you whether the goods arrived. It reads the public chain and never asks for your keys.
